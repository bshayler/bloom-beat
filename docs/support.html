Frequently asked questions

The notes feel early or late.

The game adapts its timing to your device as you play. Playing a few songs on the beat helps it settle in. Wired and Bluetooth headphones can add delay, so try again with the phone speaker if timing feels off.

How do I play hold and slide notes?

Long notes have a vine tail: press and keep your finger down until it ends. Some vines curve between lanes, so drag your finger along the vine. On the hardest songs two vines swing at once, so use two fingers.

How do I get a bigger plant?

Hit more notes and keep your combo going. Your plant is your health bar, so misses make it droop and wilt.

Where are my plants and scores saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

Which songs are in the game?

Original songs plus public-domain classics and folk tunes, played by the game's built-in synthesizer. There is a Hard mode group for experts.
