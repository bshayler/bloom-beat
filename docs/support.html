Bloom Beat Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

The notes feel early or late.

Bloom Beat adapts its timing to your device automatically. Wired or Bluetooth headphones can add delay on some devices; try playing with the speaker to compare.

Are my scores saved?

Yes. Best scores are saved on your device for every song and lane setting, and each finished song adds a plant to your Plant Shelf.

What are double vines?

On fast songs (138 BPM and up) with 4 or 5 lanes, two vines can swing at once. Use two fingers.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or in-app purchases, and the app does not send data anywhere.
