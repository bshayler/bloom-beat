Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Bloom Beat: Plant Rhythm does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

The app saves your best scores, your Plant Shelf and a timing adjustment locally on your iPhone so the game can remember your progress. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no ad or tracking identifiers
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs
  • No in-app purchases
  • No network connection is used by the app

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
