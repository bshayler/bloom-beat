Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Bloom Beat does not collect, share or sell any personal information.

What the app does with data

  • The app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.
  • It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • Your scores, plant shelf and timing setting are saved only on your device. We cannot see them, and deleting the app removes them.
  • The app plays sound and uses haptic feedback. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
