What is different

  • Real petals with curl, fold and a notched tip, not flat ovals
  • Sunflower seed head built from 480 seeds in a spiral
  • Soft light, cast shadows and a terracotta pot with a rim
  • Wilt bends the stem and browns the leaves and petals

Same stages as the game

Seedling, Sprout, Bud, Small, full flower and Big, using the game's own growth thresholds (0, 18, 34, 50, 66 and 84 percent).

About this preview

These are web concept renders, not in the app yet. The app draws its plants in 2D, so using these models there means a 3D layer such as SceneKit. The shapes here would carry over.
